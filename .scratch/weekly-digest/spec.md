# Release D: Digest replaces the weekly report

Status: resolved
Order: 6 of 6
Blocked by: savings advice

## Problem
The weekly report gives numbers that are unclear and nothing to act on.

## Design
- One plain verdict line: gas against last year, weather corrected, as a percentage. Left out
  when the data is missing.
- Top 3 open advice items with their saving.
- Measured effect of applied advice.
- No per-Room table.
- Monday, at most weekly, only when there is something worth saying.
- Bell notification plus a short push to the existing alert notify services.
- Texts in en and nl; update manuals and README.
